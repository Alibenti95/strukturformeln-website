Features

What the app does

A learning system for drug structures: content from pharmacology, three study modes, a study plan that does the planning for you, and five games that take place inside the structural formula. Everything offline, everything on the device.

Content

Over 500 structural formulas in 47 topics

The structures cover the major drug classes of pharmacology – from the cardiovascular system and the central nervous system to anti-infectives, metabolism and oncology. Every formula is a drawing from the structure data of PubChem or FDA GSRS, with molecular formula and stereochemistry.

The topics are grouped into eight areas: cardiovascular system, kidney and blood · central nervous system and pain therapy · anti-infectives · metabolism and endocrine disorders · gastrointestinal tract · respiratory tract and allergology · immunology, inflammation and oncology · further pharmacology topics.

Study modes

Flashcards, multiple choice, matching

Flashcards

One card, one structure. Flipping reveals the drug name; “Add to flashcard box” sets the card aside for later. “Knew it” or “Didn’t know” decides when it comes back.

Multiple choice

In both directions: structure → drug and drug → structure. The wrong answers are real drugs from the app’s collection, so the details matter.

Matching

A board of structures and names. Form pairs; wrong matches are briefly marked, correct ones resolve.

Custom study: pick topics on the topic wheel, pick a mode, go – independent of the study plan. If you like, it counts towards your progress too. Individual structures can be excluded from the study plan and taken back in later.

Study today

A study plan in lessons

When you create your study plan you decide which topics belong to it, what your study goal is – with a deadline, without a date or long-term – and how many lessons a day you want to study. “Study today” then guides you through new structures and due reviews in lessons; the app picks which mode comes next.

  • Four levels per structure: new, in progress, in review, consolidated – viewable as a list at any time.
  • Daily goal: lessons per day are a guide, not a limit – every further lesson starts and counts in full.
  • Study calendar: your study days with completed lessons and study time.
  • Statistics: an overview of levels, study days, lessons and study time – and the state of every single structure.
  • Flashcard box: your own collection of structures, usable in all three modes, even outside the plan’s topics.
  • Time limit: round size and time limit are set before you start in custom study; “Study today” runs without a clock.
Structural chemistry

Five structural chemistry games

All five games follow the same principle: a structure from the collection, a question, a reveal directly in the drawing. Rounds of 5, 10 or 20 questions; molecules never shown before come first. No time pressure, no points, no leaderboard.

GameTaskWhat you tap
Functional groupsWhich structural features are present? Multiple selection from four answers, atom-precise highlighting in the reveal.Answers
StereocentersFind all stereocenters; assign R/S at a highlighted centre and E/Z at a double bond – with CIP priorities and a helper view.Atoms
Hybridizationsp, sp² or sp³ for a highlighted carbon atom; or mark all carbon atoms of one hybridization. With an explanation of the bonding situation.Atoms
InteractionsHydrogen-bond donors, acceptors, aromatic rings, rotatable bonds – four question types, each with the definition used.Atoms, rings, bonds
Protonation DetectiveIdentify acidic and basic groups; at a given pH, determine the predominant form and the charged groups. Quantitative only with documented pKa values, source shown in the reveal.Groups, answers

The chemical data behind the games is generated offline with RDKit from the structure data and checked before inclusion; the app itself does no chemistry calculations. Ambiguous cases are excluded.

Look up

View all structural formulas

Search and overview

An alphabetical list of all drugs with structure previews. The search finds names and name stems – “pril” shows all ACE inhibitors, “statin” all statins. Every card: structure, molecular formula, topics.

Sources & legal

For every drug, the structure source (PubChem or FDA GSRS) with a link and, where documented, the retrieval date. For every language, the source of the displayed name and what it was checked against. Links open the source in Safari.

Display

Structures can be randomly rotated while studying, never mirrored. Stereochemistry can be hidden – then stereoisomers look the same.

Report an error

Spotted an error in a structure? “Report an error” on a card or “Report question” in a game’s reveal prepares an e-mail draft – you see the whole text and send it yourself.

Technical

Lean and offline

  • iPhone, iOS 17 or later. Portrait orientation.
  • One-time purchase on the App Store (€4.99 in Germany) – no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads. Purchase and payment are handled exclusively by Apple.
  • No network connection, no account, no sync – everything lives in the app.
  • No third-party libraries, no ads, no analytics. Privacy policy
  • Haptic feedback, can be switched off. Supports large text sizes.
  • Progress and the whole app can be reset in Settings.
