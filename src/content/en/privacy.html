2. The app

2.1 No data transmission

The app does not establish any network connection itself. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking services and no third-party libraries. There is no user account, no sign-in and no synchronisation via iCloud or other services.

The app’s privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares accordingly: no tracking, no tracking domains, no collected data types.

2.2 Data on the device

The app stores data only locally on your device, in its own storage area: the app’s settings store and files in the app container. It stores:

  • Progress: answers, ratings, levels, study time and sessions
  • Study plan: topics, exam date, minutes per day, excluded structures
  • Flashcard box
  • Settings: languages, display, haptics, time limit, round lengths

The app does not transmit this data anywhere. It is, however, part of your device backup: if iCloud Backup is switched on or you back up your device to a computer, it is included – this is done by iOS, not by the app, and Apple’s terms apply.

Under Settings → Data & reset you can reset your progress or the whole app at any time; the data is then deleted irreversibly from the device. Existing backups are not affected. If you delete the app, iOS also removes all of its data from the device.

2.3 Support and reports by e-mail

Three places in the app open an e-mail draft in your mail app. You see the entire text, can change it and decide yourself whether to send it. The app itself transmits nothing.

  • “Contact support” in Settings: an empty draft with a subject line.
  • “Report an error” on a card: details of the structure and the drug, the question shown with its answer options, your comment and the app and iOS version. Your answer and your study statistics are not included.
  • “Report question” in the five games: the game, the question’s identifier, the drug and structure, the question, the stored solution and your answer to this one question, plus your comment, the app and iOS version and the language set. Your study statistics are not included.

With every e-mail we also receive your sender address and, depending on your mail app, your name. We process the message to answer your request and to fix reported errors in the app (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We keep your message only as long as is necessary to deal with your request and any necessary follow-up, or as long as statutory retention obligations apply.

Our support mailbox is hosted by iCloud Mail, a service of Apple. Incoming e-mails are stored there and processed by Apple as the e-mail provider. See Apple’s privacy policy for details.

2.4 Links to external sources

Under “Sources & cross-checks”, links lead to a drug’s entries in external databases (for example PubChem, ChEMBL, FDA GSRS, Wikidata, RxNav, the WHO ATC index). These links open in Safari. Only when you open a link is data (such as your IP address) transmitted to the respective provider; their privacy policies apply.

2.5 App Store and reports from Apple

The app is sold exclusively through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles purchase and payment; we do not receive any payment data. What Apple processes when you download the app, make purchases or use iOS is Apple’s own responsibility; Apple’s privacy policy applies.

As the app’s provider, we receive reports from Apple in App Store Connect. This data is collected by Apple through the App Store and iOS – not by the app:

  • Sales and download figures, for example by period and country. We do not receive buyers’ names or contact details.
  • Usage statistics such as installations, sessions, active devices and crashes. They come only from devices whose users have agreed in iOS to share analytics with app developers. Apple shows them in aggregate and only once a minimum number of data points is available.
  • Ratings and reviews posted publicly on the App Store, with the name chosen there.

Whether you share analytics with app developers is set in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. We use these reports to gauge the app’s reach and to fix errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, insofar as personal data is involved).

3. This website

3.1 Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser necessarily transmits data to GitHub’s servers, in particular your IP address, the page requested, date and time, the browser used and the referring page. According to GitHub, visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the website securely and efficiently (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The transfer to the USA relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified. More information: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3.2 No cookies, no analytics, no third-party content

This website sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. There are no analytics or tracking services, no embedded videos, no social media buttons and no fonts from external servers. All content is loaded from the same address as the page itself. Only when you submit the contact form is data sent to the form service (see 3.4).

The language switch on the start page reads your browser’s language setting once to direct you to the German or English version. Nothing is stored and nothing is transmitted.

3.3 Contact

If you write to us by e-mail, we process your details to answer your request as described in 2.3.

3.4 Contact form

If you submit the contact form on the support page, your e-mail address, the topic you choose, your message and the page language are sent to the form service Formspark, operated by Trampoline Software SRL, Rue de Marsannay-la-Côte 16, 5032 Mazy, Belgium. Formspark forwards the message by e-mail to our support mailbox (see 2.3); we reply to the address you entered.

Formspark stores the data in data centres in Ireland and Germany and technically also records your IP address, an approximate location derived from it and details of your browser. According to Formspark, the IP address is kept for twelve months for spam prevention and security. For error tracking, Formspark uses the service Sentry in the USA; in the event of an error, data may be transferred there on the basis of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Formspark. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. We delete your message from the form service once it is no longer needed to deal with your request, for any necessary follow-up or for statutory retention obligations. To protect against spam, the form only uses a hidden field – no captcha and no cookies. Before you submit, the page loads nothing from Formspark.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example the one responsible for your place of residence. Since the app transmits no data to us, we usually hold no data about you – unless you have written to us.
