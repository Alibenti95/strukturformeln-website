Sources of the app’s content

The app’s structural formulas are drawings generated with RDKit from the structure data of PubChem (NCBI) and FDA GSRS. Drug names are taken from official lists of the respective countries; the complete source and licence notices are shown in the app under Settings → Sources & legal.
