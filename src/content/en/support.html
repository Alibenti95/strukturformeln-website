Feedback on a structure or a question

The quickest way is directly from the app. On a card, choose “Report an error”; in the five games, “Report question” sits in the reveal of every question. The app then prepares an e-mail with everything we need to follow up – for a game question this includes your answer to that question. You see the whole text and send it yourself. If you prefer to write here: please name the drug and briefly describe what is wrong.

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The app works fully offline and has no account. Everything you study stays on the device.

How do I create a study plan?

Under “Study structural formulas → Study today” the app guides you through it: choose topics, set your exam date and minutes per day. You can change the plan later under “Edit study plan” or in Settings.

Can I exclude individual structures from the study plan?

Yes. From a running task in “Study today” via the “…” menu, or in the study plan itself. Excluded structures can be taken back in there.

How do I change the language?

Settings has two separate options: “App language” for the interface and “Flashcard language” for the drug names. Switching only changes the display – progress, flashcard box and statistics are kept.

How do I reset the app?

Under “Settings → Data & reset”: “Reset progress” deletes only your progress and keeps settings, flashcard box and study plan. “Reset the app completely” deletes everything the app has stored. Neither can be undone.

Where do the structures come from?

From the structure data of PubChem and FDA GSRS, drawn with RDKit and cross-checked against ChEMBL and Wikidata. In the app, “Sources & cross-checks” shows the structure source and the origin of the names for every drug.

Which device does the app run on?

The app is designed for iPhone and runs on iOS {{ios_minimum}} or later.
